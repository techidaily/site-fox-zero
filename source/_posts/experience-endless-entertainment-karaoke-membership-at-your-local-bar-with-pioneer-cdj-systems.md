[Karaoki](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) also automatically manages singer rotation, so whomever is put in charge of manning the software during your karaoke night can focus on keying in a searches for song requests and adding them to the singer rotation instead of keeping track of where everyone is in order.
